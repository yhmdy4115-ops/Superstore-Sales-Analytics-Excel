# 📊 US Superstore Sales & Performance Analytics (2016–2019)

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics and dashboard design project using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales, profitability, shipping modes, and customer segments for the **Sample Superstore** dataset spanning from 2016 to 2019.

The goal of this project is to evaluate business performance, identify top-performing product categories and regions, analyze profitability margins, and present executive insights via an dynamic dashboard.

---

## 🛠️ Key Technical Highlights & Excel Skills
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Cleaned raw transaction records, calculated order periods, profit margins, shipping durations (days to ship), and categorized sales metrics.
- **Advanced Formulas & Functions:** Leveraged dynamic functions (`SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `IF`, `VLOOKUP`/`XLOOKUP`, `TEXT`) for dynamic KPI calculations.
- **Pivot Tables & Analytics:** 
  - **Region x Category:** Cross-regional sales analysis.
  - **Category Breakdown:** Unit sales, transaction counts, and revenue percentages.
  - **Shipping Mode Efficiency:** Revenue share across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day.
  - **Quarterly Trends:** Tracked historical growth trajectory over 4 years.
  - **Top 10 Products:** Identified highest revenue-generating products.
- **Executive Dashboard Design:** Built an interactive **Management Performance Dashboard** with summary cards (Total Sales, Total Profit, Avg Profit Margin, Top Region) and custom slicer filters.

---

## 📊 Summary of Key Findings
- **Total Sales & Profitability:** Generated high-volume revenue across 4 primary regions (West, East, Central, South), with **Technology** and **Office Supplies** maintaining strong profit margins.
- **Shipping Modes:** **Standard Class** represents the dominant shipping method, accounting for the highest proportion of transactions and revenue.
- **Regional Performance:** The **West** and **East** regions continuously lead overall sales volume.
